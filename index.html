About KII'26

Kumaraguru Intra Ideathon

An intensive 36-hour sprint curated exclusively for first-year engineering cohorts in interdisciplinary squads of 4 to 5 members. Departing from rigid single-theme constraints, KII'26 offers 105 rigorously framed industrial and societal problem statements spanning diverse scientific circles.

Structured into multi-tier evaluation benchmarks: from formulation to rigorous technological validation.

Compliance & Standards

Rules & Guidelines

Participants are required to maintain strict adherence to academic rigor, integrity, and safety protocols throughout the 36 hours.

• Exclusively open to registered First-Year B.E. / B.Tech students of Kumaraguru College of Technology (Batch 2026–2030).

• Teams must strictly consist of 4 to 5 members. Inter-departmental collaborations are strongly encouraged.

• All proposed solutions must represent authentic, original research conceived for KII'26. Plagiarism in code, abstracts, or designs results in immediate disqualification.

• A team can register under one primary Research Circle from the 7 circles provided.

• Valid Kumaraguru College of Technology Student Identity Card (Mandatory for entry and check-in).

• Personal laptops, charging adapters, surge protectors, and 3-pin extension cords per team.

• Basic electronic kits or specialized test components (if applicable to your selected circle statement).

• Notebooks, pens, and personal essentials suited for the overnight campus session.

• 24/7 institutional campus security rules apply. Leaving the designated MGate hackathon perimeter without written authorization from organizers is strictly prohibited.

• Zero tolerance for academic misconduct, unauthorized code reproduction, or disruption of fellow teams.

• Respect for laboratory machinery, spatial infrastructure, and collegiate cleanliness protocols.

• On-campus security personnel, faculty coordinators, and medical helpdesks are stationed 24/7.

• Punctual attendance is mandatory for all three milestone reviews (Review 1, Review 2, Final Review).

• Every team member must present during the final jury defense.

• Standard institutional dress code must be strictly maintained across both days of the ideathon.

• KII'26 delegate identification badges must be visible at all times within the venue.
